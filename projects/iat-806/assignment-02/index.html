BODY BRIDGE

A body-controlled physics puzzle where you become part of the game world! Use your webcam to capture your silhouette, turn yourself into a physical obstacle, and guide a falling ball into its target. Experiment with different poses, gravity, and momentum to find your way to victory.

Notes

BODY BRIDGE is an experimental game developed with p5.js and ml5.js BodyPix for IAT 806. The project explores how human movement and body silhouettes can become interactive elements in a virtual physics environment. By transforming webcam-based body segmentation into collision surfaces, players can create ramps, barriers, and unconventional paths for a falling ball. The game currently uses frozen silhouettes and simplified collision physics, with planned experiments involving multiple silhouettes, recorded movements, and increasingly challenging levels.
