Lab 05 — Drift & Stir

An interactive particle-painting experiment inspired by painting on water, where colorful droplets drift, grow, settle, and respond to mouse interactions.

Notes

Built with p5.js using object-oriented programming, this experiment explores particle movement, inertia, and interactive manipulation. Left-clicking releases particles that gradually slow down and expand, while right-clicking drags existing particles and middle-clicking creates swirling motions. The brush continuously transitions between random pastel colors, encouraging users to wait for their desired shade. By combining simple behaviors, the particles form evolving compositions that resemble pigments suspended in water. Rather than simulating realistic fluid physics, the project explores how individual objects and simple interactions can produce complex visual patterns.
