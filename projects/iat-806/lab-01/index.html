Lab 01 — Drawing with primitive shapes

An illustration of a study room in the afternoon.

Notes

I only used circles and rectangles to create the illustration. I avoided using variables, functions etc. to study the hard way first. I am planning to revisit the idea later when we learn the advanced concepts. I also tried to use the color palette from the reference image as much as possible. I used a lot of trial and error to get the right colors and positions for the shapes.
