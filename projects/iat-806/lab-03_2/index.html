Lab 03 — One Piece of Caramel

An interactive pixel-art dance experience that remixes One Piece characters with the playful rhythm and visual energy of Caramelldansen.

Notes

The project begins with a short animated intro before transitioning into a looping dance scene synchronized with music. Viewers can switch between different One Piece characters using the left and right arrow keys, while the entire scene bounces rhythmically to reinforce the arcade-like, playful feel. The visual language combines retro pixel-art aesthetics, simple interaction, and character-based animation into a lighthearted fan-made experience.
