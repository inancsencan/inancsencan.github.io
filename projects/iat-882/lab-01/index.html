Signal Machine

An interactive audiovisual generative system based on L-systems.

Audio Mapping

1.0×
1.5 s

Notes

Press P to start, pause, or resume the audiovisual system. Hold H to display the debug HUD and audio analysis values. Press R to reset the generative visual state.

Use the Audio Mapping controls to assign audio features to Rule Probability, Generation Timing, Vertical Displacement, Line Thickness, and Color. Additional controls adjust line thickness, temporal fade duration, and the gradient colors.
